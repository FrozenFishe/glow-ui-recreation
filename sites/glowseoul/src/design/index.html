How it was made

  • Photoshop 2026 (ExtendScript): content-aware fill under a computed UI mask gave a clean hero photo; named user slices for the hero, six category thumbnails and the flag, exported with Save for Web.
  • slicer.py + tune.py: 22 icons and 27 text lines traced to vectors, sub-pixel tuned, with original-pixel cutouts for the tiniest.
  • glyphfont.py: three fonts built from the design itself: GlowSeoul Serif (logo), GlowSeoul Display (headline), GlowSeoul Italic (“Australia”). fontmatch.py picked Inter for the body text.
  • tune_shapes.py + fit_pills.py: button and cards fitted to 1/8 px; the frosted pills solved as colour + opacity over the plate.
  • inkgain.py: per-element hairline strokes from a 5-width sweep. fit_code.py: live-text size, spacing and offsets fitted over 5 render rounds.
  • exact_layers.py: the original as a background plus 8 named section layers.
